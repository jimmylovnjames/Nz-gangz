AOTEAROA: UNDERWORLD | RUN 19 (HOW IT PLAYS: MOVEMENT, FIGHTING, THIRD PERSON, SOUND)

Extract this ZIP and keep the voice folder next to index.html. Open index.html on your phone with internet on.
Give the progress bar a few seconds on first load, then tap NEW GAME.
Photo textures, the photo sky and the realistic people stream in after it loads (about 22 MB the first time, then cached).
Use wifi for the first run. Without internet it falls back to the generated look and stand-in people.

The map is real Manurewa and Clendon Park (OpenStreetMap): real streets and names, every house on its real outline,
Southmall with the Norfolk pine, the station, the Clendon shops and Clendon skate park. The HUD shows the street you're on.

RUN is a toggle: tap it once (it lights up) and steer with your other thumb; it switches off when you stop.

NEW IN RUN 19
- PAUSE > VIEW switches to THIRD PERSON (over the shoulder, you can see yourself). PAUSE > SOUND turns sound off.
- Fights: they circle and take turns swinging; your hits stagger them; health bars over their heads.
  Tap BLOCK while moving to DODGE (can't be hit during it). Aim assist turns you onto the nearest one.
- Movement has momentum and real speeds. Footsteps, punches, the till and your phone all make sound.
- A light beacon marks your objective. Banners show what you earned. Get knocked out or busted and you get another go.

NEW IN RUN 18
- HEAT: hit randoms, run people over, ram cop cars or brawl in public and the cops come (stars on the HUD).
  Stay next to a cop car and you're busted (night at Manurewa station, 30% fine). Get away and lie low to lose them.
- Six new car types (hatch, sedan, wagon, Hilux-style ute, people mover, Hiace) and NZ Police cars.
- People walk the footpaths and scatter when things kick off.
- CHAPTER ONE after the crew chapter: Tipene's run to Southmall with Manu riding shotgun, Aunty Lani's dairy,
  the rival crew at the skate park, and Manu getting jumped at the station.

NEW IN RUN 17
- Cars: walk up to any parked car and tap TALK to get in. Joystick drives (up = go, down = brake/reverse,
  left/right = steer). HIT is the horn. TALK again to get out. Traffic drives on the left and stops for you.
- Shops: walk up to a dairy, takeaway, bakery, supermarket, pharmacy, barber or the gym and tap TALK.
  Food heals, energy drinks give a full-sprint boost, the gym makes your punches hit harder.
- Fighting: watch them wind up, then BLOCK (hold the button) or step back. Every third hit is a heavy.
  Health creeps back to 60 on its own; eat for the rest. Get knocked out and you lose 10% of your cash.
- After Shorty's chapter he keeps texting new jobs (name, amount, street). Sort them for a 25% cut.

You start on Manu's couch in Clendon playing Kiwi Knuckle Kings 2 on his old TV.
Fight pad: arrows to move, BLOCK, SPECIAL, KICK, PUNCH. SKIP ends the match early.
Tap the subtitle box to skip ahead in a conversation; tap a reply to choose it.
After the match Manu tells you about Darren and the $400. Offer to collect it, find Darren, then take the money back to Manu.
Manu then passes your number to Shorty (he's over in Manurewa). Watch for the phone button (top right): MESSAGES and JOBS.
Shorty's three jobs: Kev (scrap), Mikey (he runs, tap RUN to chase him), Uncle Pita (your call).

Voice work: VOICE_SCRIPT.md lists every line with its id. Record each line as its own file,
drop the files in the voice folder and list them in voice/manifest.js, e.g.
  window.AU_VOICE = { pro_01: 'pro_01.m4a', pro_02: 'pro_02.m4a' };
Or just send me the recordings named by id and I'll wire them in.

PAUSE (top right) has GRAPHICS / TIME / WEATHER and SHOW FPS. Use HIGH or MEDIUM for the realistic people.
Older saves still load; if a save is from before the real map you continue outside Manu's place.
